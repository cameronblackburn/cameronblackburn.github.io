Bio

I'm a Computer Science student with a particular interest in algorithms, formal languages, and how programming languages are built. I like projects that force me to actually understand the theory rather than just apply a library — building things like automata engines and interpreters from scratch.

My coursework has covered algorithm complexity and correctness proofs, formal language theory and automata, and combinatorial optimisation (metaheuristics, local search, graph colouring). I'm currently looking for software engineering roles where I can keep building on that foundation.

What I've been working on
